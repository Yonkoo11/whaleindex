Why the agent held.

“All three portfolios pointed the same direction — long, and heavily so. The risk officer will not let the index take a one‑sided bet that large. So this cycle, the most disciplined move was no move at all.”

Every candidate breached the same guardrail: the index would have been 100% directionally biased against an 80% ceiling, and in two cases over‑concentrated in a single coin. The Coordinator chose to hold. No USDC moved. That restraint is the product — a passive whale index that refuses to chase a crowded trade is doing exactly its job.
